## What defines it
- **B-roll carries the story; the voice runs underneath.** Every location change is *shown* as a
  mini shot sequence (grab door -> drive -> step out -> arrive) instead of a jump cut.
- **Location names are the titles.** Giant condensed uppercase (Anton-like), warm yellow #F4E23C,
  stacked, fitted edge to edge, top third, often with the subject in front of the letters.
- **Subtitles are quiet:** small serif (Instrument Serif-like), soft yellow #F6E05E, sentence case,
  lower-middle, full phrase at once. Hero words are NOT popped; they become titles instead.
- **Cuts on the word:** "here / here / here" gets one location each; "one location / to another"
  cuts on "another".
- **Practical transitions:** hand over the lens, objects passing the lens, rack focus, whip pans.
- **Film-like grade:** natural colour, gentle S-curve, warm highlights and cool shadows, no glow.
- **Collages** for "my videos / my work": 2x2 grid of past thumbnails with condensed labels.

## Shot list to give the user (for a cinematic reel)
1. Macro action that starts the move (door handle, keys, shoes, coffee).
2. Travel POV (driving, walking feet, window).
3. Low-angle arrival (camera on the ground, they step into frame).
4. Wide establishing with the subject small - leave the top third clear for the title.
5. 2-3 detail tilt-ups (trees, buildings, signage).
6. Talking head at each location, medium and close.
7. A "hand over the lens" ending to each location, for the transition.
Film everything in 4K (or at least 1080p) at 24 or 30fps, vertical, with the camera app's
highest quality setting, not a screen recording.
