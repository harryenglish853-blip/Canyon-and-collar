# 14 — EVENING RETREAT

**Reserved negative space:** upper band / deep blue sky

## Intent

Blue hour. The sky has gone deep and clean, the last warmth draining off the canyon rim. Low
path lights come up along the decomposed-granite walk — warm, shielded, pointed at the
ground. The tall suite windows glow amber from inside and read as the warmest thing in frame.
The concierge and the hero dog walk away from camera down the path toward the building, both
unhurried, the leash slack and swinging. Their silhouettes are dark against the lit glass
they are walking toward.

the building opens up around them. No camera shake.
