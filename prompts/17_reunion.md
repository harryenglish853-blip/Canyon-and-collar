# 17 — REUNION

**Chapter:** REUNION  ·  **Scroll position:** 96.5% → 98.5%
**Deliverables:** `17_reunion`
**Format:** 2.39:1  ·  **Lens:** 50mm  ·  **Duration:** 5-8s  ·  **Light:** late afternoon, warm and open
**Reserved negative space:** upper left

## Intent

The same entrance court as 02, closing the loop. The owner comes through the gate. The dog's
whole body changes in one frame — the head lifts, the ears come forward, the tail goes. It
crosses the stone at a fast trot, not a movie-slow-motion gallop, and shoves its head
straight into the owner's hands. The owner is already down on one knee by the time it
arrives. The concierge stands back, holding the leash, and lets the moment belong to them.
Real, slightly messy, over in seconds. No tears, no swelling strings, no slow-motion.

## Overlay typography (composited by the website — do not render into the asset)

> **WELCOME HOME.**

## Camera

50mm, f/2.0, gently stabilised handheld with a real human weight to it — the only handheld
shot in the film. Soft backlight behind the pair.

## Motion

Follow the dog across the court, settle low as the owner kneels, and hold. Let it run
slightly long and end on stillness rather than on the peak of the excitement.

## Transition

Out on the pair holding still together. Pull back for the final hero.

## Audio bed

fast paws on stone, one single happy vocalisation, a laugh, collar hardware, the music
dropping out almost entirely for two seconds
