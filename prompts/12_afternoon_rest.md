# 12 — REST / AFTERNOON NAP

**Chapter:** REST  ·  **Scroll position:** 75.0% → 80.0%
**Deliverables:** `12_afternoon_rest`
**Format:** 2.39:1  ·  **Lens:** 85mm  ·  **Duration:** 6-8s  ·  **Light:** interior, filtered daylight
**Reserved negative space:** upper left

## Intent

The suite, quiet. The dog is fully asleep on the linen bed, on its side, one front paw
extended past the edge, flank rising and falling. A long warm bar of sun lies across the bed
and the floor, dust turning slowly in it. A loose linen curtain at the window lifts and
settles on a breath of air, sliding the light a few centimetres across the dog and back.
Nothing else in the frame moves. The room feels like a room where nothing bad happens.

## Camera

85mm, f/1.4, extremely shallow — the dog's shoulder and ribs sharp, the far side of the room
dissolved.

## Motion

Very slow dolly-in over the full duration, no more than a quarter metre total. Breathing,
curtain, light, dust. Nothing else. Resist every urge to add a beat.

## Transition

Out slowly. Cut to the widest exterior of the film for 13.

## Audio bed

deep slow dog breathing, a curtain touching plaster, distant muffled birdsong, near-silence,
one low piano note held and allowed to decay
