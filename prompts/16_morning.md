# 16 — MORNING LIGHT

**Chapter:** MORNING  ·  **Scroll position:** 94.0% → 96.5%
**Deliverables:** `16_morning`
**Format:** 2.39:1  ·  **Lens:** 50mm  ·  **Duration:** 5-7s  ·  **Light:** early morning, clean cool-warm light
**Reserved negative space:** upper right

## Intent

The same suite, the same bed, a different hour. Clean early sun comes in low and lays a
hard-edged warm rectangle across the floor and up onto the bed. The dog wakes the way dogs
actually do — eyes open first and stay still, then a huge front-leg stretch with the chest
dropped and the back arched, a shake that starts at the ears and runs to the tail, tail
already moving. The suite door opens. A concierge steps in with the leash over one shoulder.
The dog is up before the door has finished swinging.

## Overlay typography (composited by the website — do not render into the asset)

_none — pure image_

## Camera

50mm, f/2.0, clean commercial look, slightly cooler in the shadows than the afternoon
material to sell the hour.

## Motion

Slow dolly-in that begins on the bar of light on the floor and rises to find the dog as it
stretches. Settle wide enough to take the open door and the incoming staff member in one
frame.

## Transition

Out on the dog heading for the door. Cut exterior to 17.

## Audio bed

birds, a shake with the collar rattling, nails on stone, a door, the theme restating lightly
