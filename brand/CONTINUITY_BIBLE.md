# Canyon & Collar — Continuity Bible

Every asset must feel filmed at the same property, on the same day, with the
same dog. Continuity is what turns 19 clips into one stay.

The block below is pasted verbatim into every generation prompt in `prompts/`.
If you regenerate a single shot months from now, paste it again.

---

## The locked block

> SAME PROPERTY THROUGHOUT: a single-storey desert-modern retreat of warm
> sandstone block, weathered vertical cedar slats, hand-troweled sand-coloured
> plaster, deep shaded overhangs, and tall bronze-framed glass. Native planting
> only — sage, juniper, desert grasses, mesquite, agave, decomposed-granite
> paths edged in stone. SAME HERO DOG: one calm, elegant Golden Retriever with
> a mid-gold coat, slightly darker ears, a quiet expressive face, wearing an
> aged brown leather collar with matte brass hardware and a small brass tag.
> SAME STAFF WARDROBE: soft olive or charcoal overshirt, cream or khaki
> trousers, dark brown leather belt, no logos, no scrubs. SAME PROPS: aged
> brown leather leash, matte brass hardware, cream stoneware bowls with a thin
> charcoal rim, oatmeal linen bedding, natural jute and wool textures.

---

## Architecture

- Single storey. Long, low, horizontal. It sits into the slope, never on top of it.
- Warm sandstone block walls, coursed, with visible joints.
- Weathered vertical cedar slats for overhangs, screens and soffits.
- Hand-troweled plaster in sand, inside and out. Texture visible in raking light.
- Deep overhangs — the shade line is the building's strongest graphic element.
- Tall bronze-framed glass, floor-to-ceiling or knee-to-ceiling. Never small windows.
- Honed stone floors inside. Decomposed granite outside, edged with stone.
- No visible bars, mesh, chain link, or hardware that reads as containment. Anywhere.

## The suite (04, 05, 09, 10, 11, 12, 15, 16 all play here)

The same room every time. Geography must not drift:

- Doorway on the **left**, opening to a corridor.
- Tall window on the **right of centre**, looking onto planting and canyon rock.
- Low pale-oak platform bed with an oatmeal linen mattress, left of centre, on a wool rug.
- Oak ledge with the two cream stoneware bowls, right, under the window.
- Small open storage nook, far right, holding the folded blanket and the toy.
- A single small blank brass plate on the door jamb. This is where branding goes later.
- Beyond a second glass door, a private walled patio with one shade sail and a juniper.

## The hero dog

One Golden Retriever across every key scene. Mid-gold coat, slightly darker
ears, quiet expressive face, calm default posture.

If a second-unit shot cannot use the hero dog, it must be a detail shot where
the dog is not identifiable — paws, a flank, a muzzle at the water bowl.

Companions appear only in 07, and only two: an Australian Shepherd and a French
Bulldog, well spaced.

## Props, and they are the same props

| Prop | Description | Appears in |
|---|---|---|
| Collar | Aged brown leather, matte brass hardware, small brass tag | all |
| Leash | Aged brown leather, matching brass snap hook | 02, 03, 06, 16, 17 |
| Bowls | Cream stoneware, thin charcoal rim, pair | 04, 08, 09, 12, 15 |
| Bedding | Oatmeal linen over a low oak platform | 04, 10, 12, 15, 16 |
| Owner's blanket | Worn plaid, muted rust, creased from home | 05, 10, 15 |
| Owner's toy | Soft, genuinely used, fur licked flat on one side | 05 |
| Food container | Labelled, owner-supplied, with a measuring scoop | 05, 09 |
| Notes card | Handwritten feeding notes, always angled away from legibility | 09, 10 |

Worn, not new. The leather has a darkened patch where it has been held. The
brass has micro-scratches. The blanket keeps its creases.

## Wardrobe

Soft olive or charcoal overshirt, sleeves rolled. Cream or khaki trousers. Dark
brown leather belt. Quiet boots. No logos, no name badges, no scrubs, no
lanyards, no branded caps.

## Grade

One grade across all assets: warm, open shadows, retained highlight rolloff,
gentle natural saturation. Skin and fur keep their real colour. No teal, no
crushed blacks, no bleach bypass, no filter.

## The chain of transitions

the scroll:
